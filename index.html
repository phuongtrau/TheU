Making sense of an irrational world.

Watch on YouTube

The Unrationalist is a YouTube channel of debate-style video essays on psychology, decision-making and economics. In every episode two fictional characters argue a question: Mr. U, who trusts intuition, and Dr. R, who trusts reason. Either of them can be wrong; the published evidence decides each round, and every factual claim is sourced in the video description.
